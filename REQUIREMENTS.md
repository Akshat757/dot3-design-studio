## 4. Process — "How we work" (6 steps)

1. **Understanding the requirements** — project requirements, expectations, vision and preferences.
2. **Conceptual designs & massing** — after the first discussion, develop concepts and an initial planning draft.
3. **Finalizing the design** — refine concepts through iterations with client feedback.
4. **Visualizing the design** — 3D renders for client visualisation and adjustments before moving ahead.
5. **Documentation** — GFC (good-for-construction) drawings and documentation.
6. **Execution** — execute the approved design with periodic inspections for quality and workflow.

Good fit for a scroll-animated timeline on the landing page.

## 7. Brand & visual direction (from the decks)

- Palette: warm greige `#D8D5D0` (panels), off-white `#F5F3F1` / `#FAF9F7` (backgrounds), soft grey `#EFEDEB`, near-black text, muted terracotta/brown accent on headings (approx. `#8B5E4A`, confirm from the logo).
- Type: v1 uses **TAN Moncheri** (thin display serif, headings) + **Montserrat** (body). TAN Moncheri is a commercial font: the studio needs a web licence, or we use a free lookalike (e.g. a thin high-contrast display serif from Google Fonts). Montserrat is free.
- Layout motifs: wide letter-spacing, vertical rotated section labels ("portfolio", "our vision"), framed photos with white borders, black-and-white team photos, a three-dot motif (the logo dots), a spiral/concentric-circle graphic on the contact page. Calm, editorial, architectural.
- Animation should stay in this calm luxury register (reveals, parallax, masks, slow transitions), not loud.
