# "Find your needs" questionnaire — draft for client approval

One question per screen, progress bar ("4 of 15"), Back/Next, answers kept if the page is refreshed. Most questions are tap-to-choose so the form takes about 3 minutes on a phone.
Questions adapt to the answer in Q1: the services list (Q3) and the spaces list (Q8) change for residential vs. commercial/hospitality. Questions marked _optional_ can be skipped.
After Q15, a short contact step (not counted in the 15), then a review screen and **"Send on WhatsApp"**.

---

## Part A — The project

**1. What are you planning to build or design?** _(single choice)_
Home / bungalow · Apartment / flat · Farmhouse / villa · Office / commercial space · Shop / showroom · Hotel / restaurant / hospitality · Factory / industrial · Plotted development / township · Other (type)

**2. Where is the site?** _(single choice + text)_
Indore · Ujjain · Elsewhere in Madhya Pradesh (type town) · Outside MP (type city)

**3. What do you need from us?** _(multiple choice)_
Architecture (planning & building design) · Elevation / façade design · Interior design · Landscape design · Custom furniture · Turnkey execution (design + build) · Not sure yet — guide me

**4. What stage is the project at?** _(single choice)_
Just bought the plot / still searching · Plot ready, nothing designed yet · Drawings exist, need redesign or second opinion · Construction ongoing · Structure complete, need interiors · Existing space to renovate

**5. How big is the site or space?** _(number + unit, optional)_
Plot area ___ sq ft · Built-up / carpet area ___ sq ft · Floors planned ___ · "I don't know yet" checkbox

## Part B — How you'll use it

**6. Who will use the space?** _(single choice + number)_
Residential: Family of ___ members · includes elderly / children / pets (checkboxes)
Commercial / hospitality: Approx. ___ staff / rooms / covers

**7. How would you like the space to feel?** _(pick up to 3)_
Calm & serene · Warm & earthy · Bold & statement-making · Minimal & clean · Luxurious & grand · Close to nature · Practical & low-maintenance

**8. Which spaces matter most to you?** _(multiple choice, list depends on Q1)_
Residential: Living / drawing room · Kitchen & dining · Master bedroom · Kids' room · Pooja room · Home office · Guest room · Terrace / balcony · Garden / courtyard · Pool · Parking
Commercial / hospitality: Reception / lobby · Work areas · Cabins · Conference room · Guest rooms · Restaurant / café · Showroom floor · Storage / back-of-house

**9. Which design style draws you?** _(pick up to 2, each with a small image from DOT THREE's portfolio)_
Contemporary modern · Classical / contemporary-classical · Minimal · Bohemian / earthy · Indian traditional · Industrial · Tropical / resort · Not sure — show me options

**10. How important is Vastu?** _(single choice)_
Must follow strictly · Follow where practical · Not important

**11. Anything specific you want to include?** _(multiple choice + text, optional)_
Natural light & ventilation · Double-height space · Courtyard · Water feature · Smart-home automation · Solar / sustainable features · Lift · Home theatre · Gym · Lots of storage · Other (type)

## Part C — Budget & timeline

**12. What is your approximate budget for this scope?** _(single choice)_
Under ₹25 lakh · ₹25–50 lakh · ₹50 lakh–1 crore · ₹1–2 crore · Above ₹2 crore · Not decided — need guidance
_(Bands to be confirmed by the studio.)_

**13. When would you like to start?** _(single choice)_
Immediately · Within 1–3 months · 3–6 months · Later / just exploring

**14. Do you have reference images or ideas?** _(optional)_
Paste a Pinterest / Instagram / Drive link · or "I'll share on WhatsApp" checkbox
_(A static site cannot upload photos into WhatsApp, so photos are sent in the chat afterwards.)_

**15. Anything else we should know?** _(free text, optional)_
e.g. existing problems, family needs, deadlines, must-avoid items.

## Contact step (required, not counted)

Full name · Mobile number (prefilled +91, validated) · Preferred way to talk: WhatsApp call / Phone call / Visit the studio · Best time to reach you: Morning / Afternoon / Evening

## Review screen

All answers listed, each with an "Edit" link back to that question. Buttons: **Send on WhatsApp** (primary) · Copy answers · Start over.
Small note: "This opens WhatsApp with your answers filled in — just tap Send."
